# Which results carry over?

**No solver in this checkout advances mass, momentum and total energy for compressible viscous water.** The repository does contain genuine fluid solves. Their equation sets and evidence determine what we can borrow.

## Solver choice for physical runs

Use an established transient, conservative finite-volume package with pressure–velocity coupling accurate at very low Mach number, an implicit acoustic step for slow evolution, true physical sound speed and an energy-consistent liquid EOS. Retrofitting the mixed FEM fixtures would require new unknowns, thermodynamics, fluxes and time integration. The proposed round-two reference needs **no CFD package**. A published all-speed pressure-based method demonstrates the relevant combination of conservative updates, viscous flow and real-fluid acoustic propagation. Its results do not certify this vortex. [Denner, Evrard & van Wachem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10482)

For a later physical pilot, a concrete candidate **if licensed access exists** is Fluent's transient pressure-based coupled solver in double precision with its NIST single-phase liquid-water model. Inspect the exact version, water backend, viscous work, pressure work, conduction, flux output, wall stresses and initial-state import before choosing it. License/access and accuracy here are unverified. For an open-source candidate, SU2 documents CoolProp integration but restricts nonideal convective schemes. Its low-Mach options cannot simply be assumed compatible with Water. A package that cannot resolve the pressure signal, account for total energy or reproduce acoustic propagation fails qualification. No package acquisition is needed for this review.
