# Can the installed solver step over sound waves in rotating water?

**SU2/CoolProp can execute a physical dual-time step. Its tested 25 µs step fails.** We kept the medium disk, water restart, transport, JST flux and dissipation coefficients identical. One backward-Euler physical step used implicit pseudo-time updates, pseudo-CFL 10, a relative linear-solver tolerance of 10⁻⁸ and a cap of 50 linear iterations per update. Each trial was independent. Full solver logs, convergence rows and configuration files accompany the fields. [Measured campaign](rotating-disk/fields.zip)

| Physical interval | Method | Inner solves | Peak pressure change | Kinetic change per metre |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12 ns | Four explicit midpoint steps | — | 1.2912 Pa | −2.070 pJ |
| 12 ns | One backward-Euler step | 8 | 1.2889 Pa | −2.108 pJ |
| 25 µs | One backward-Euler trial | 12 | 61,047 Pa | **+397 µJ** |

At 12 ns, log₁₀ RMS density residual dropped from −6.43 to −11.60 and plateaued. Momentum rose initially and then plateaued near −8.01. The dual-time result reproduces the explicit pressure pattern to similar peak magnitude. Core radial velocity RMS was twice the explicit measurement. At 25 µs, log₁₀ RMS x-momentum residual went from −6.84 to **+0.77**, with 5–10 linear iterations per pseudo-step. The inferred 30 mm pressure rise became −20.4 kPa, its fourfold symmetry was broken by up to 3.46 kPa, and the total-energy inventory gained 2.73 mJ/m. Independent recovery classified all stored endpoint states as liquid. The final state is a failed inner iterate. The program exited successfully on reaching iteration caps. Physical-time convergence, even first order, remains unestablished. A tolerance or step refinement around a divergent iterate cannot supply a physical diffusion measurement. ## What the executable actually does

1. The installed HEOS object updates from (ρ,e), recovers pressure, temperature and physical sound speed, and evaluates the pressure and temperature partial derivatives. Transport in the tested configuration uses constant molecular water values. The nonideal centered dispatch selects the general compressible viscous implementation, including heat conduction, viscous work and temperature derivatives. The successful 12 ns pilot exercises the actual library, face-flux and linear-solver paths together. [HEOS implementation](https://github.com/su2code/SU2/blob/7048fd35f56d4bf52e586429d6746a36a10255f8/SU2_CFD/src/fluid/CCoolProp.cpp#L44-L69) · [Viscous dispatch](https://github.com/su2code/SU2/blob/7048fd35f56d4bf52e586429d6746a36a10255f8/SU2_CFD/include/numerics_simd/CNumericsSIMD.cpp#L95-L132)
2. For a fixed mesh the dual-time routine adds V(U−Uⁿ)/Δt or V(3U−4Uⁿ+Uⁿ⁻¹)/(2Δt) to the conservative residual, with the corresponding diagonal Jacobian term. BDF2 restart loads two physical levels. It would require a converged startup step and a time-step convergence study. Only the first-order physical path was run. Direct implicit Euler dispatch is present, but a linearized update by itself does not demonstrate a converged nonlinear physical step. [Physical-time source](https://github.com/su2code/SU2/blob/7048fd35f56d4bf52e586429d6746a36a10255f8/SU2_CFD/include/solvers/CFVMFlowSolverBase.inl#L1658-L1709) · [Second-order history](https://github.com/su2code/SU2/blob/7048fd35f56d4bf52e586429d6746a36a10255f8/SU2_CFD/include/solvers/CFVMFlowSolverBase.inl#L1100-L1143)
3. The centered inviscid residual uses recovered primitive pressure and enthalpy. The **implicit face Jacobian** in that same path calls a constant-γ formula. For example, its momentum derivative with respect to conservative energy contains (γ−1)n, where the general-EOS pressure derivative is (∂p/∂e)ρ n/ρ. JST scalar-dissipation Jacobians also take γ. An approximate Jacobian can converge to the correct residual root. The mismatch supplies a concrete suspect for the poor large-step solve. The trials do not isolate its effect from pseudo-time damping, conditioning or the incompatible initial mass flux. [Centered flux and Jacobians](https://github.com/su2code/SU2/blob/7048fd35f56d4bf52e586429d6746a36a10255f8/SU2_CFD/include/numerics_simd/flow/convection/centered.hpp#L137-L145) · [Matrix entries](https://github.com/su2code/SU2/blob/7048fd35f56d4bf52e586429d6746a36a10255f8/SU2_CFD/include/numerics_simd/flow/convection/common.hpp#L280-L310)

## Method decision for eventual compressible evolution

Keep the installed explicit binary as a short-window reference. Retire the tested dual-time configuration as a production candidate at 25 µs. Repairing SU2 would require checking liquid-EOS Jacobian actions against finite differences of the **same** momentum, energy and dissipation fluxes, scaling residuals for liquid compressibility, and demonstrating a converged physical root with step halving. The wider grid reduces one source of startup sound while the implicit operator must still resolve physical pressure forces.

For a future solver decision, prefer a conservative pressure-based acoustic-implicit formulation with a general-EOS pressure solve. Boscheri and Pareschi split advective and acoustic fluxes, treat pressure implicitly through the energy equation, and use a nonlinear pressure solve for general EOS. Their published scheme includes viscous stress and Fourier heat flux. Adapting it to liquid water requires a verified single-phase e(ρ,p) inversion and derivatives at each pressure iteration, recovered sound speed c²=(∂p/∂ρ)ₛ, matched momentum/enthalpy fluxes, and conservative exchange across refinement interfaces. Its Cartesian formulation suits a periodic box. Curved rigid walls and local refinement would need independently checked flux treatment. The paper establishes a method architecture. This host has no verified IAPWS implementation of it in this campaign. [Primary method and equations](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.01789)

Acceptance for a water implementation would require the recorded acoustic amplitude and phase gates with resolving physical steps, shear dissipation at both phases, then the disk's radial residual, startup pressure, vorticity decay and energy budget. Repeat the physical step at half size and tighten the pressure/nonlinear residual tolerance tenfold. Large steps can filter unresolved sound even with the correct EOS. Compare resolved pressure and slow vorticity rates to the acoustic-resolving baseline before extending time. The installed binary has shown no stable, converged slow-time route that meets these conditions. [Existing quantitative gates](RING_EVOLUTION_DESIGN.md#qualification-before-a-pair-run)
